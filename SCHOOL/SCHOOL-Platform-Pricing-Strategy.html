Financial waterfall

GST is not a business expense here: output GST is removed from GST-inclusive customer collections. Input tax credits and cash remittance are not modeled. Expenses are a share of ex-GST sales. Execution strategy

01 · Validate offers

Test ₹999 entry access, quarterly conversion, annual commitment and restricted lifetime passes. Distinguish self-paced content from live coaching.

02 · Build acquisition

Use content, webinars, referrals, communities and employer partnerships. Track traffic, conversion, acquisition cost and refunds by source.

03 · Protect unit economics

Budget for instructors, support, hosting, payment fees, content production, marketing and customer success. Track cohort contribution margins.

04 · Diversify revenue

Consider corporate learning licenses and group-coaching cohorts separately from this five-pass B2C simulation. Do not double count revenue.

Accounting and legal caveats

The 25.168% tax starting point is an illustrative Indian domestic-company Section 115BAA rate, subject to eligibility and professional verification. LLPs and proprietorships can be taxed differently. GST classification, place of supply and exemptions must be confirmed. Lifetime prepaid access may require deferred revenue and a future-service liability. ₹6 crore company profit after tax is not the same as ₹6 crore distributed personally to the owner.
