## Features

- Clickable membership cards and direct checkout links
- Editable membership prices
- Balanced, premium, entry-focused and single-plan simulations
- ₹6 crore annual after-tax company profit target, adjustable from ₹1–12 crore
- GST, company tax and operating expense sensitivity
- Required sales, annual/monthly purchases, visitor-to-purchase conversion funnel
- Responsive, self-contained HTML with no external dependencies

## Cautions

This is an illustrative transaction-level model, not a forecast. It does not model renewals, upgrades, churn, refunds, deferred revenue, platform commissions separately, or owner-level distribution taxes. Confirm corporate tax and GST with a qualified Indian accountant.
