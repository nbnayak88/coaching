HUB growth strategy

Entry → upgrade

Use Fast Start as the acquisition offer; measure conversion to Silver, Gold and Diamond. Avoid counting upgrades as new unique learners.

Premium positioning

Clearly differentiate Gold and Diamond coaching, access duration, support and benefits. Do not promise perpetual services without costing them.

Acquisition funnel

Measure visits, checkout starts, purchases, CAC, refunds and upgrades by channel. Replace illustrative conversion assumptions with actual HUB analytics.

Profit discipline

Include mentor delivery time, platform commissions, payment fees, marketing, content and support. Reconcile collections against recognized revenue.

Assumptions and limitations

Official starting prices from the supplied HUB service listing (annual billing): Fast Start Bundle / Bronze L0 — ₹599 per year; Silver L1 — ₹5,999 per year; Gold L2 — ₹49,999 per year; Diamond L3 — ₹1,49,999 per year. The Fast Start title mentions lifetime membership, although the listing shows yearly billing; confirm the entitlement terms. Tax assumes an illustrative eligible Indian domestic company; verify legal entity, Section 115BAA eligibility, GST applicability and revenue recognition with a chartered accountant. Profit after company income tax is not personal post-distribution take-home income.
